HOW FLYING WORKS

THE 4 FORCES OF FLIGHT

A plane stays in the sky because four forces push and pull on it at the same time. Meet them all!

THE 4 FORCES

  • F1Lift
  • F2Weight
  • F3Thrust
  • F4Drag
F1LIFTpushes UP ↑

The UPWARD force that holds the plane in the air.

  • Wings push air down, so the air pushes the wings up.
  • Faster flying = more lift.
F2WEIGHTpulls DOWN ↓

The DOWNWARD force that pulls the plane toward Earth.

  • It is caused by gravity.
  • Plane + fuel + people + bags = total weight.
F3THRUSTpushes FORWARD →

The FORWARD force that pushes the plane through the air.

  • Engines or propellers push air backward.
  • So the air pushes the plane forward!
F4DRAGpulls BACK ←

The BACKWARD force from air pushing against the plane.

  • It is air resistance – it slows the plane down.
  • A smooth, pointed shape has less drag.

WHEN THE FORCES BALANCE

LIFT > WEIGHT

Extra lift → the plane CLIMBS

EQUAL FORCES

Lift = Weight and Thrust = Drag → STEADY level flight

THRUST > DRAG

Extra thrust → the plane SPEEDS UP
