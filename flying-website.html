THE FOUR FORCES

A force is a push or a pull. Two of them work up and down, and two work forward and backward. Let's meet them one by one.

HOW THE FORCES WORK TOGETHER

The forces come in two pairs: Lift vs Weight (up and down) and Thrust vs Drag (forward and backward). Which one is bigger decides what the plane does. Try it!

F1 Lift vs F2 Weight

F3 Thrust vs F4 Drag

Lift is greater than weight
The plane climbs. Pilots tilt the nose up a little or fly faster to get more lift.
Thrust is greater than drag
The plane speeds up. Pilots open the throttle to make the engines push harder.

When both pairs are equal, the forces cancel out and the plane cruises at a steady height and speed. That is what you feel on most of a long flight.
